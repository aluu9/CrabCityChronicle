How it works
Filing a Report
All entries are submitted through the Mafia Clash Discord using slash commands. Join the server and use any of the following:

/submit-player — your kills, deaths, rank, family, clan
/submit-war — war result between two clans
/submit-clan-history — a clan event or milestone
/submit-news — a news post or rumour
/submit-patch — a dev update

Every submission enters a mod review queue. Nothing goes public until a moderator approves it. You will receive a Discord notification either way.
How War Score is Calculated
War score determines clan ranking on the board. It is calculated from each submitted war result:

Win+3 pts
Loss+0 pts
Kill differential (per 10 kills ahead)+1 pt
Clean sweep (opponent 0 kills)+2 pts bonus

Scores accumulate from all submitted war entries. Use /submit-leaderboard to update your clan's running totals. Mods verify numbers before they count toward the ranking.

Player Stats — Video Evidence Required
Record a video of your in-game stats screen. Your username and all stats must be visible with no cuts. Use the Discord bot to submit — upload your video in Discord and use the /submit-player command.
What your video must show:
▸ Your in-game username clearly visible
▸ The full stats screen open
▸ All stats readable — Time Played, Kills, Deaths, Wins, etc.
▸ No cuts or edits — minimum 5 seconds on the stats screen
▸ Max 50MB — MP4, WebM, or MOV
How to submit:
1. Record your screen showing the stats page
2. Upload the video to any channel in our Discord
3. Right-click the video → Copy Link
4. Run /submit-player and paste the link in the video_url field
